Privacy Policy

LUNA89 Fakture Backup is a private backup utility used by the owner of the LUNA89 Fakture invoicing application.

Information processed

The utility processes backup archives containing application data and uses Google Drive authorization to upload, list, verify, and manage backups in the owner's Google Drive account.

Purpose and storage

Backup files are stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the owner. They are not published on this website.

Retention

The intended retention period is 30 days. Older backups are deleted according to the configured retention rules after a successful new backup.

Access and sharing

The utility is for private use. It does not sell backup data or intentionally share backup contents with third parties. Google processes data as the storage provider under its own terms.
